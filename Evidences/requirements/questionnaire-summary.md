# Questionnaire Summary

## Hospital Appointment Booking System

### Overview

A questionnaire with 15 questions, based on simulated Google Forms, was developed to determine what the users might expect to find in the Hospital Appointment Booking System.

For academic requirement analysis, an artificial data set of 25 responses is used.

## Major Findings

### 1. Current Appointment Booking

The findings show that the traditional way of making appointments is still prevalent. 72% of simulated respondents reported that their primary means of contact was by phone or face-to-face at the hospital reception desk.

In addition, 80% reported regular or occasional problems with appointment booking.

This supports the need for a more convenient online appointment-management process.

### 2. Dr. Search and Specialization

The average usefulness rating for online doctor search was 4.48 out of 5, and 88% said it would be useful to filter doctors by specialty.

These findings support:

* **FR-04 — Search Doctor**

### 3. Doctor Availability

The mean rating for available appointment slots was rated at **4.64/5** for viewing available appointments.

This was a very solid finding and a strong support:

This action presents a list of available appointment slots.This action displays the list of available appointment slots.

### 4. Appointment Management

The questionnaire revealed high levels of support for the appointment management functions:

* **92%** supported online cancellation.
* **88%** supported online rescheduling.
* **96%** supported appointment confirmation.

These findings support:

* **FR-08 — Book Appointment**
* **FR-10 — Appointment Confirmation**
* **FR-12 — Cancel Appointment**
* **FR-13 — Reschedule Appointment**

### 5. Usability

The ease of use was rated an average of **4.60 / 5** for importance.

This supports:

* **NFR-03 — Usability**
* **NFR-10 — Accessibility**

### 6. Security

The highest average was for security (4.76 out of 5).

This supports:

* **NFR-01 — Security**
* **NFR-09 — Data Integrity**

## Conclusion

The results of the simulated questions confirm the major functions suggested for the Hospital Appointment Booking System. The doctor's availability, doctor appointment management and booking, confirming appointments, usability and security are of special importance for the users.

When defining final system requirements, the findings are taken into account in conjunction with the simulated stakeholder interview and other requirement analysis procedures.
