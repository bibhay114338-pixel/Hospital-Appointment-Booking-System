# Stakeholder Analysis

Stakeholders are people or groups who interact with the proposed system, are influenced by it or have an interest in the successful development and operation of it.

The stakeholders of the Hospital Appointment Booking System are given below.

| Stakeholder            | Type      | Role                                          | Main Interest/Requirement                                              | Influence |
| ---------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Patients | Primary| Main users of appointment services, easy search for doctors, easy booking, canceling and rescheduling of appointments | High      |
| Doctors                | Primary   | Healthcare providers                          | Accurate appointment schedules and appointment details                 | High      |
| Hospital Administrator | Primary   | Administrative system user                    | Manage doctors, schedules and appointment records                      | High      |
The Reception Staff: Secondary: Support hospital appointment operations: Access to organized appointment information: Medium.
| Hospital Management    | Secondary | Oversees hospital operations                  | Improved efficiency and reduced scheduling problems                    | High      |
| Project Team           | Internal  | Plans, analyzes, designs and tests the system | Successful completion of project requirements                          | High      |
Instructor/Supervisor  | External  | Reviews and evaluates the project  | Correct application of software project-management practices  | High      |

## Patients

The booking system is primarily used by patients. They need a system in place to easily locate doctors who would suit them, check availability, make appointments, and manage their current appointments.

### Main Needs

Easy to register and log in.
* Easy doctor search
* Accurate appointment availability
* Fast appointment booking
* Ability to delete appointments
* Knowledge of the procedures for rescheduling appointments
* Clear booking confirmation

## Doctors

Accurate and updated appointments schedules are essential for doctors to have so they can plan their time and be ready for appointments with patients.

### Main Needs

* Secure login
* View appointment schedule
The information is shown to be relevant to an appointment.
Correct and up to date timetable data

## Hospital Administrator

The hospital administration is in charge of keeping doctor and appointment details.

### Main Needs

Add and update Doctor info
* Manage doctor availability
* View appointment records
* Manage appointment information
Keep up-to-date system information

## Reception Staff

Reception staff can assist patients with activities involving appointments. They are not included as a main system actor in this work, but their workflow is impacted by the appointment management process.

## Hospital Management

Hospital management is eager to optimize its operations, minimize conflicts and enhance the entire appointment management process.

## Project Team

The project team is charged with analyzing the requirements, planning using Agile, modeling with UML, testing, documenting, managing the project on Trello, and version controlling using GitHub.

## Instructor/Supervisor

Instructor oversees the academic project, reads the chosen topic, assesses the project results and guides the project.
