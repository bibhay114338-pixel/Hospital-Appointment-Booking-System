# Functional Requirements

Functional requirements are used to outline the functions and services the proposed system is to provide in the Hospital Appointment booking System.


| ID | Functional Requirement | Description | Primary Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | Patient Registration | The system shall enable a new patient to register with the required personal and login details.| Patient | High |
| FR-02 | User Login | The system should be able to accept valid login details of registered patient, doctor and administrator. | Patient, Doctor, Administrator | High |
| FR-03 | Patient Profile Management | The patient shall be able to access their basic details on the patient profile management system and be able to update them. | Patient | Medium |
| FR-04 | Search Doctor | The System should enable patients to search doctors. | Patient | High |
| FR-05 | Filter Doctor by Specialization | The system will enable the patient to filter doctors based on their medical specialization.  | Patient | High |
| FR-06 | View Doctor Information | The system should be able to retrieve relevant information relating to a selected doctor. | Patient | Medium |
| FR-07|View Available Appointment Slots |The system shall list available dates and time slots for a particular doctor's appointments.  |  Patient | High |
|FR-08|  Book Appointment| The system will enable Patient to book an available appointment with a selected doctor. | Patient | High |
|FR-09 | Prevent Duplicate Booking | The system shall not allow the same Doctor, Date and Time to be booked twice. | System | High |
|FR-10|Appointment Confirmation|The system shall confirm an appointment after successful booking. | System, Patient | High |
|FR-11|View Appointments | The system shall enable patient to view their scheduled appointments and appointment status. | Patient | High |
|FR-12| Cancel Appointment|The system shall provide patients with the ability to cancel eligible appointments.  | Patient | High |
|FR-13|Rescheduele Appointment|The system shall enable patients to reschedule an eligible appointment to a different date or time. | Patient | High |
|FR-14| View Doctor Schedule|The system shall enable doctors to see their schedules. | Doctor | High
|FR-15|View Appointment Details |The system shall enable doctors to see the pertinent information about appointments booked for their doctor. | Doctor | Medium |
|FR-16 | Manage Doctors | The system should enable the administrator to add, view and update the doctor information. |  Administrator|High |
|FR-17|Manage Doctor Availability| The system will let administrator create and update doctor's available appointment schedules. | Administrator | High |
|FR-18|Manage Appointments| The system shall enable administrator(s) to view and manage the appointment records. | Administrator | High |
|FR-19|User Logout|The system allows authenticated users to securely log out.The system should enable patient, doctor and administrator to log out safely from the system.| Patient, Doctor, Administrator | Medium |

## Summary
These functional requirements are the primary activities of the proposed system. They are used as the basis for developing user stories, UML diagrams, and software test cases.
